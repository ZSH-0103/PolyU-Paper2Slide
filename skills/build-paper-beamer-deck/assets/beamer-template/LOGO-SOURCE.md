# PolyU white bilingual lockup

The source is page 36 (physical PDF page) of the university's [School of Fashion and Textiles undergraduate prospectus](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/sft/-/media/department/sft/publication/academic-programmes/ug-prospectus-2026_digital_1.pdf). The upper white mark consists of the interlocking emblem, the English university name in two lines and 香港理工大學. The source outlines were extracted without the printed background or the separate school logo. The lockup is centered without stretching inside the template's approved picture rectangle.
