# Final quality checklist

## Source and narrative

- Claims were checked against the methods and evaluation, not just the abstract.
- Metrics retain their definitions, units, baselines, and evaluation setting.
- Proposed deployments and future directions are distinct from demonstrated results.
- Image crops match the paper, preserve relevant labels, and exclude printed captions.
- Figure numbers are correct. Short captions are faithful summaries; full captions remain in notes or a source map.
- The order explains the argument for this audience. Outline and section names agree when an outline is present.
- Repeated figures or explanations have an intentional purpose.
- PhD framing does not invent the person's broader research program or individual contribution.

## Format and timing

- The speaking duration is confirmed for this presentation. The slide count, including the cover, fits the time budget and any user-supplied page limit.
- A long paper talk follows the paper’s section order. A short paper talk concentrates on its main idea and evidence.
- Short talks have no unnecessary outline or standalone title/thank-you slide.
- Script word count, speaking-rate assumption, slide timings, and pauses are plausible.
- Timing is labeled an estimate until rehearsed or recorded.
- A slide deck is described as preparation for a video, not a completed video submission.

## Visual review

- The template's canvas, red strips, footer, page count, and typography are respected.
- The important figure details and text are readable at presentation size.
- Captions appear once and stay separate from figures.
- No text, figures, or notes intrude into the footer or neighboring columns.
- Every final slide has been rendered and individually inspected after the last material change.

## Technical delivery

- XeLaTeX uses `ctex` with `fontset=fandol`.
- No LaTeX/package errors or unresolved overfull boxes. Underfull warnings are visually reviewed.
- Beamer PDF and source correspond to the same final build.
- Required source assets are included in a portable source bundle.
- If PPTX was requested, it is a real editable PPTX, with native text and intact figures, and its final output is also visually inspected.
- Private build logs and intermediate renders are kept out of the deliverables directory.

- Verify Red section labels and slide titles are both Arial 20.3 pt, bold and left-aligned in PowerPoint. Verify PolyU Blue titles have one section prefix separated by a centered dot, including the cover and closing.
